For camps, youth organizations, charities, NPOs, community and recreational organizations, and faith-based organizations, volunteers are essential to fulfilling the mission. A consistent volunteer reference checking process can help organizations make more informed selection decisions while strengthening their broader approach to safeguarding. Volunteers are at the heart of many mission-driven organizations.
